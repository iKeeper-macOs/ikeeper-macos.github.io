FAQ

Is iKeeper secure?

Yes. Passwords and sensitive data are stored locally with macOS-native encryption. iCloud syncing uses end-to-end security.

Does it work on Apple Silicon?

Fully optimized for M-series chips and macOS 26, including liquid glass UI, dark mode icons, and toolbar enhancements.

How do I import old data?

Use the built-in importer for iKeeper 2 files. It handles Internet and product records seamlessly.

What’s the sidebar for?

Quick access to saved searches and frequent items. Toggle it on for always-ready navigation.
